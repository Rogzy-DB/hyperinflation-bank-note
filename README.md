# Hyperinflation Archive

Every major hyperinflation since 1921 as a sourced case study: the context, what happened, life during it, how it ended,
the banknotes and the data. Plus the record before 1921, and a library to go further.

**Live: [hyperinflation.rogzy.org](https://hyperinflation.rogzy.org)**

## What is on the site

- **20 hyperinflations and one contrast case** (Turkey 1990–2005, chronic inflation that never reached 50% a month),
  on one timeline, grouped by era
- **A case study per period**: key figures, the exchange-rate curve, the events and the banknotes on one line, then
  context, life during it, how it ended, lessons, the data and the sources
- **44 banknotes** from David St-Onge's collection, with what each one bought in its month (where the series allows)
- **And even before**: France 1795–96, the Confederate and Continental dollars, Kipper und Wipper, Yuan/Ming paper money, Rome
- **A library** of 33 books, descriptions and covers from Plan B Network's open educational content (CC BY-SA 4.0),
  with 15 legal free editions linked
- **Open data**: JSON and CSV per case, `data/all.json`, one banknote ZIP per case, `llms.txt`, a citation per case
- Every page is complete static HTML: no framework, no JavaScript needed to read it, charts drawn as SVG

## How it is built

```
data/periods.json            the periods: figures, dates, banknote labels
data/eras.json               the eras the home page groups them in
data/before.json             the home quotes and the pre-1921 record (every line sourced)
data/exchange-rates/*.json   chart series; their notes are shown under the charts
data/library.json            the Library (generated by fetch-library.py, committed)
content/<id>/info.md         each case's text (generated by export-knowledge.py, never edited by hand)
assets/bills/                banknotes: originals/, web/ (1200 px), thumbnails/ (300 px)
assets/library/              book covers (from Plan B's repo)
site/style.css               the one stylesheet
scripts/build-site.py        builds everything into dist/
```

```bash
python3 scripts/export-knowledge.py <knowledge-dir>   # case texts; --check reports drift. Anything marked unverified is left out
python3 scripts/fetch-library.py                      # refresh the Library from Plan B's repo (network)
python3 scripts/process-images.py                     # after adding a banknote to assets/bills/originals/ (needs Pillow)
python3 scripts/build-site.py                         # → dist/
cd dist && python3 -m http.server 8000                # preview
```

`dist/` is not committed: it is built and copied to the server as is (any static file server works; old
`/pages/<id>.html` links redirect to `/<id>/`).

**Adding a period:** add it to `data/periods.json` and an era in `data/eras.json`, its banknotes to `assets/bills/originals/`
(then `process-images.py`), its text in the knowledge base (then `export-knowledge.py`), optionally a sourced series in
`data/exchange-rates/<id>.json`, and rebuild. Every figure carries its source.

## Data sources

- Banknote images: David St-Onge's collection, used with his permission. **Not covered by the MIT licence.**
- Monthly peaks and episode dates: Hanke & Krus (2012), *World Hyperinflations*, Cato Working Paper no. 8.
- Histories: cited per period (Hanke & Krus, Wikipedia articles, academic papers named in each text).
- Exchange-rate charts: sources and caveats shown under each chart; approximate series say so. Angola and the
  Philippines have no chart until a sourced series exists.
- Library: [Plan B Network, bitcoin-educational-content](https://github.com/PlanB-Network/bitcoin-educational-content),
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (titles and punctuation corrected, as the site says).
